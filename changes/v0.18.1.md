# v0.18.1

## Highlights

Nushell's plugin management commands are now available in handlers and `eval` scripts: `plugin list`, `plugin stop`, `plugin use`, and the rest of the `plugin` family, so plugins bundled with the embedded Nushell can be inspected and controlled at runtime.

Added regression coverage for streaming disconnect cleanup: a `.cat --follow` or `.last --follow` handler whose SSE client disconnects now has its thread released promptly rather than parking until the next matching frame arrives. This exercises the fix shipped in cross-stream 0.14.1 (the dependency bumped in v0.18.0) and guards against it regressing.
